I'm a full stack developer in Lyon. I like things simple, well structured and well made. My mantra: “Keep it simple, stupid”.

After a two-year degree in Valence and a master's in computer science at Université Savoie Mont Blanc, I joined Sopra Steria in 2016. I spent four years there, starting with Java and Spring Batch, then becoming technical lead and lead developer on a team of around fifty people across France and Spain: code reviews and audits, estimates, and welcoming junior developers.

I went freelance in 2020. Since early 2021, I've worked for **Le Monde** on [Sirius](https://www.sirius.press), the CMS the group's newsrooms use to write and publish their articles. I work on the editor, real-time collaboration, the API and everything that keeps production running.
